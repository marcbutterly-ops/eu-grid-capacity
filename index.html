Method and bands

Spare capacity is each grid operator's own published figure for new demand, at the finest level it publishes. Spain is shown by transmission node (220 kV and 400 kV), Great Britain by primary substation, Sweden by county, the Netherlands by high-voltage station, and France by designated zone.

Sweden publishes band values only, and its lowest band is shown here as under 25 MW. Great Britain primary substations are distribution sites, so a 100 MW site would usually connect at a grid supply point; grid supply point headroom is summarised in the overview table. In Merseyside, Cheshire and North Wales, spare capacity is estimated as firm capacity minus peak load. Granted and requested demand is capacity already granted plus requests in progress, shown in bands.

Germany is shown by transmission area as demand and renewable supply, since no spare capacity is published. Ireland is shown by county: transmission-connected renewables and the published data-centre position. Belgium, Finland, Norway, Italy, Portugal and Morocco are in the overview table at country level.
