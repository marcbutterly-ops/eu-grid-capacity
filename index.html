European grid capacity for data-centre siting

Spare capacity for new demand, from each grid operator's own publication, at the finest level each one publishes. Spain has spare capacity at of its transmission nodes. Sweden has 100 MW or more in of its counties. In the Netherlands, of high-voltage stations are short of capacity for new demand.

Capacity is shown in bands and summarised by region. Substation-level detail, names and locations are available on request. Data as of 16 November 2026.

Method and bands

Spare capacity is each grid operator's own published figure for new demand, at the finest level it publishes. Spain is shown by transmission node (220 kV and 400 kV), Great Britain by primary substation, Sweden by county, the Netherlands by high-voltage station, and France by designated zone.

Sweden publishes band values only, and its lowest band is shown here as under 25 MW. Great Britain primary substations are distribution sites, so a 100 MW site would usually connect at a higher level. Granted and requested demand is capacity already granted plus requests in progress, shown in bands.

Belgium, Finland, Norway and Italy are in the overview table at country level. Ireland and Germany are in progress.
